# Three-minute demo recording plan

Target duration: **3:15**. Whitechain requires 2–4 minutes and each party must be visible. Use cuts or pause/resume for network waits. Do not accelerate narration, fake confirmations, hide rejected actions as successful, or present offline guest content as a live model run.

## Prepare before pressing Record

1. Keep company/vendor services, gateway, tunnel and the bounded launch operator running as required. Use a company you control and retain its browser cookie. Do not show terminal passwords, keys, .env files or signed journals.
2. Finish company/employee provisioning before the main take. Registration involves 33 transactions for all three roles and can exceed the whole video budget. Capture the real progress separately if desired; speed it up with an explicit “Registration wait condensed” caption, or show its completed transaction checklist and say it was prepared earlier.
3. Save the actual business description, product, audience, language, shipping/return policies and contact in Company. If undecided, write “Not confirmed; do not promise it.” This avoids a missing-fact question interrupting the short take. Do not invent offers to make the demo faster.
4. Use your existing confirmed INR 500 credit; do not pay again just to repair the display. To film a *new* Razorpay checkout, the company needs at least 5 extra uncredited test USDT. Record its test success flow once and pause while the webhook settles. Existing receipt footage is acceptable if clearly described as an already captured test payment.
5. Prepare a Finance proposal and, if time is tight, approve it before the main take. Capture the approval separately. Do not replay an approval on a completed proposal. Normal complete-campaign allocation is INR 30 Marketing / INR 10 Support / INR 0 Finance; review the actual proposal rather than assuming these values.
6. Use one original mission, not repeated submissions. Keep the mission below the five-mission company quota. Configure real model mode locally for the founder demo; the public guest is intentionally offline.

## Handling waits honestly

- Keep approvals visible: founder budget consent and above-threshold purchase approval are part of the product, not an inconvenience to edit away.
- Cut only the waiting period. A small caption can say “Testnet wait condensed; same saved request.” Keep transaction links visible afterward.
- If a founder question appears, answer with actual facts, pause while it resumes, then continue. Do not consume the recording with silent waiting.
- If a service fails, fix it before the final take. Do not claim a queued job is settled.
- Keep the raw recording. Upload the edited 2–4 minute video publicly or unlisted with access for judges, then add the link to docs/SUBMISSION.md and the showcase. This repository does not claim an uploaded video exists yet.
