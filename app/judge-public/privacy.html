Your data in this demo.

EnsWhite is an experimental agent-company demo. Please use fictional business information and test payment details.

What the app stores

Cookies select your guest or company workspace. The browser also saves your chosen light or dark theme locally. The service may store business descriptions, missions, messages, employee wallets, approvals, payment identifiers, deliverables and reviews. Guest sessions expire after an hour; real testnet company records and their transaction journals can remain on the operator’s server.

Connected services

Razorpay handles test checkout. In configured AI mode, relevant business context and tool results are sent to the configured model provider. Public guest missions use offline content and simulated payments.

What is public

ENS names, onchain addresses, transactions, identity metadata and published reputation feedback are public. Blockchain records cannot be removed by deleting a browser cookie. Do not put private customer data, API keys or passwords in business briefs or reviews.

Questions

Contact the project through GitHub issues. Share public transaction links when useful; never post secrets.
