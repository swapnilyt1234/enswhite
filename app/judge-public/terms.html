TESTNET DEMO · UPDATED NOVEMBER 7, 2026

A workspace for testing.

EnsWhite demonstrates an agent company using Sepolia identities, Whitechain testnet payments and Razorpay test checkout. It is an experimental demo, not a production financial service.

Test payments only

Fiat payments are simulated. Test USDT and test WBT have no monetary value. The fixed demo allocation is ₹500 of sandbox payment for 5 test USDT of company credit from a prefunded pool. No real-money conversion occurs.

Approvals and automation

Review the team, budgets and purchase approvals before confirming them. The backend operates service wallets within configured limits. Founder reviews are published through a service reviewer wallet. You do not need a browser wallet for the supported onboarding flow.

Content and availability

Review generated copy, translations and business policies before using them. Model output can contain mistakes. The demo has daily launch limits, mission quotas and dependencies on external testnets. Work can pause when funding or a network is unavailable.

Use fictional data

Do not enter real payment details, private customer records or secrets. For support, open a project issue without posting private information.
